About the NZ Learner Licence test

What's on the test?

The Class 1 Learner Licence test in New Zealand is a 35-question multiple-choice exam. You have 30 minutes to answer, and you need to get at least 32 out of 35 questions correct to pass. Questions are pulled from the official NZTA question bank, which is publicly available — so practice questions like the ones in TrafficNZ are very close to (and often identical to) the questions you'll see on test day.

Where do you take it?

You sit the test at an AA driver licensing agent, a VTNZ branch, or an NZ Post agent (specifically the ones licensed for driver testing — call ahead to confirm). The test is on a tablet, with multiple-choice answers you tap. Your photo and signature are captured at the same appointment so your physical licence card can be printed afterward if you pass.

What do you need to bring?

  • Proof of identity — an NZ passport, a foreign passport, or a combination of accepted documents (birth certificate + current photo ID).
  • Proof of address if your address isn't on your ID.
  • The fee (around $48 at the time of writing — confirm at nzta.govt.nz for the current amount).
  • Glasses or hearing aids if you usually wear them — you'll do a quick eyesight test as part of the booking.

What happens after you pass?

You get a Learner Licence, which means you can drive only with a supervisor in the front passenger seat. Your supervisor must have held a current and valid NZ full driver licence for at least two years and have it with them when supervising. You also need to display L plates on the front and back of any vehicle you're driving.

After six months, you can sit the Restricted Licence practical test. After 18 months on Restricted (or 12 if you've done an approved defensive driving course), you can sit the Full Licence practical test.

What if you fail?

You can rebook the test as soon as you like — usually the same day. Most test centres can refit you within a week. The fee applies each time. Most candidates who use a study aid like TrafficNZ pass first try; those who go in cold typically need 2–3 attempts.

About Apphive

Apphive is an independent project building small, focused web apps that solve one problem well. We believe most software is bloated and over-engineered. Our apps do one thing, do it cleanly, and respect your time.

Everything we build is browser-based and mobile + desktop friendly. There are no logins, no tracking pixels beyond basic analytics, and no subscriptions. Where we run ads (like Google AdSense on this site), it's to cover hosting and keep the apps free.

How we work

  • TypeScript and modern web standards. No legacy frameworks.
  • Static hosting where possible — fast, secure, cheap.
  • Real official content (Crown Copyright Road Code, Wikimedia Commons sign artwork) — not AI-generated mockups.
  • Built and tested by a team of two in New Zealand.

Privacy policy

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

Apphive itself does not collect personal information. We do not require accounts, and our apps store user data (such as quiz progress in TrafficNZ) in your own browser using localStorage — not on our servers.

We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns. This involves first-party cookies that record anonymised information such as page views, browser type, and country. You can opt out via your browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
